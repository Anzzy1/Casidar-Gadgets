FAQ

Questions people ask us a lot

Still curious? Send us a message and a real person will reply.

Do you sell brand-new units too?

Yes. Most of our units are pre-owned, but we also have brand-new ones. Choose "Brand new" in the request form and tell us the model you want.

What's a "Wi-Fi only" iPhone?

It's an iPhone that works on Wi-Fi but doesn't connect to a SIM network, so you can't use it for calls, texts or mobile data. Everything else works: apps, camera, Messenger and video calls on Wi-Fi. It's a budget-friendly choice, and we always tell you clearly if a unit is Wi-Fi only.

Do you have older iPhone and iPad models?

Yes. Since most of our units are pre-owned, we often have older iPhones, iPads, minis and Airs. They're great as a first phone, for kids, or as a backup. Just tell us the model you want.

How does pre-order work?

Tell us the exact model, storage, color and budget. We get the unit ready and message you with real photos. Then pick it up at the branch, or have it delivered.

Are your units openline?

It depends on the unit. Some are openline, some are Wi-Fi only, and some are network-locked. We always tell you which before you decide.

Will I see the battery health?

Yes. We include it when we message you, and you can check it yourself in Settings when you visit. You can also set a minimum (for example 85%+) in your request.

Do you have a warranty?

Yes, our units come with a shop warranty. It covers defects that aren't caused by the user. Physical damage like a cracked or broken screen, drops, or water damage isn't covered. Ask us about the warranty period for the unit you're getting.

Do you deliver or ship?

Yes. We offer 24-hour delivery, and we ship nationwide via LBC. Choose your option in the request form and we'll tell you the rates and details.

How do I pay?

Not through this website. For pick-up, you pay at the branch after checking the unit. For delivery or LBC shipping, we'll send you real photos of the unit first and tell you how to pay. We accept cash, GCash and Maya. Ask us about other options.
