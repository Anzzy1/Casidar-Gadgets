# Casidar Gadgets: Website Plan (v2)

## Business
- Sells **pre-owned iPhones and iPads only**
- Main store: Brittany 1, Antipolo City. Branch: Gold Land, Cainta
- Stock depends on each batch, so there is **no product listing**. Customers **ask** or **pre-order**.
- No online checkout

## Page structure
1. Topbar showing whether each branch is open right now
2. Hero: "Pre-owned iPhone. Checked like it's new." Three real photos that widen to full width as you
   scroll, with floating glass badges (battery health, iCloud-clean, Face ID)
3. Scrolling list of the models we source
4. Bento grid: iPhone, iPad, "Why no product list?" with a scrolling model list, Trade-in
5. **Request builder** (the main feature): Ask/Pre-order tabs → device → series → model → storage
   (and connectivity for iPad) → condition and battery minimum → budget slider and color → details and branch.
   A live "ticket" summary sits beside it and updates with every choice.
   Submitting opens a dialog to send the message via Messenger, SMS, Viber or email.
6. Quality check: a photo with an animated scan line, plus 6 check cards
7. How pre-order works: 4 steps with a progress line that fills as you scroll
8. Trade-in banner
9. Branches (map, address, directions, call, "Ask this branch"). No store hours; they open flexibly, often until midnight
10. FAQ (why no list, pre-order, openline, battery health, warranty, payment)
11. Footer with a photo background that moves as you scroll, and an Apple trademark disclaimer

## Motion
Scroll-driven showcase expansion, photo parallax, fade-ins as you scroll, a progress line, magnetic
buttons, cursor glow, glass badges that follow the mouse, and animated chips and ticket values. All of it
turns off for people who set "reduce motion" on their device.
